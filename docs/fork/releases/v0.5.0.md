**Sauce plays your own Steam copies of Portal 2, Portal Stories: Mel, Portal, Half-Life 2 (with Episode One,
Episode Two and Lost Coast) and Left 4 Dead 2, on iPad, iPhone and Mac.** No game files are in this download:
the Natif Installer builds everything on your Mac from the games you own.

## Install

1. Install your games in **Steam** on your Mac, as usual.
2. Download **Natif-Installer-v0.5.0.dmg** below, open it and drag **Natif Installer** to **Applications**.
   Then open it like any other app: it is signed and notarized by Apple.
3. If it asks, click **Install…** next to “Xcode command line tools”.
4. Pick the **iPad and iPhone** tab or the **Mac** tab. Each game found in Steam is listed. If a game shows a
   `download_depot` line, click **Copy**, then **Open Steam console**, paste the line, press Return and wait
   for “Depot download complete”: the game turns ready by itself.
5. **iPad or iPhone:** plug it in, unlock it, pick it, then click **Install**. **Mac:** click **Build**.

The first time takes a while: building on your Mac, then copying 5–14 GB per game to the device. Your saves
on the device are backed up to the Mac before anything there is replaced.

| Game | Steam console line (its Linux files) | Download | First Mac build |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portal 2 | `download_depot 620 661` | about 90 MB | about 10 min |
| Half-Life 2 (+ Episode One, Episode Two, Lost Coast) | `download_depot 220 224` | about 320 MB | about 20 min |
| Portal | `download_depot 400 404` | about 150 MB | about 10 min |
| Portal Stories: Mel | `download_depot 317400 317404` | about 90 MB | about 15 min |
| Left 4 Dead 2 (offline only) | `download_depot 550 554` | about 510 MB | about 20 min |

The Mac games need Apple silicon. Building Sauce for an iPad or iPhone needs Xcode and your Apple ID (a free
one works; a free ID's copy is refreshed every 7 days with one click in the installer).

## New in this release

- **Every game installs from the installer, on both tabs.** Left 4 Dead 2 was missing: it now builds for the
  Mac and installs into Sauce on iPad and iPhone like the others, with its own touch layout.
- **Every Steam library is scanned** on both tabs, including libraries on another disk.
- **Half-Life 2's episodes and Lost Coast** show on the Mac tab when they are installed.
- **Signed and notarized:** the installer opens with no extra step.
- Also in Sauce since v0.4.0: the series launcher, TV output, touch controls with a layout editor, controller
  support, a phone as a controller, Community Maps and the Workshop on iPad.

Left 4 Dead 2 is offline only: single-player campaigns with bots, never Valve's servers. Portal 2:
Community Edition is not in the installer yet.
