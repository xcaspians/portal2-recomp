**Sauce plays your own Steam copies of Portal 2, Portal Stories: Mel, Portal, Half-Life 2 (with Episode One,
Episode Two and Lost Coast) and Left 4 Dead 2, on iPad, iPhone and Mac.** No game files are in this download:
the Natif Installer builds everything on your Mac from the games you own.

## Install

1. Install your games in **Steam** on your Mac, as usual.
2. Download **Natif-Installer-v0.5.2.dmg** below, open it and drag **Natif Installer** to **Applications**.
   Then open it like any other app: it is signed and notarized by Apple.
3. On the **iPad and iPhone** tab, step 1 **Setting up tools** checks this Mac. If Apple's command line tools
   are missing, their installer opens by itself: click **Install** there. Click **Set up tools** if it lists
   anything; click **Get Xcode** or **Open Xcode** if it asks for Xcode (it's free).
4. Each game found in Steam is listed. If a game shows a `download_depot` line, click **Copy**, then
   **Open Steam console**, paste the line, press Return and wait for “Depot download complete”.
5. **iPad or iPhone:** plug it in with a cable (faster and more reliable than Wi-Fi), unlock it, pick it,
   then click **Install**. **Mac:** click **Build**.

## New in this release

- **Quitting stops everything.** Quitting the installer used to leave its build and copies running in the
  background, and the next launch then sat on “Waiting” until the Mac restarted. Now Cmd+Q asks first when
  something is running and stops all of it; even a Force Quit or a crash takes it down. The next launch
  cleans up anything an older version left behind.
- **Waiting always says why**, in the window and in the details: the earlier run it waits for and when it
  started, with **Stop it and start again** when that run has no window any more.
- **Copies survive a dropped connection.** If the iPhone or iPad locks (iPhone Mirroring keeps it locked) or
  drops off Wi-Fi, the copy says so (“Your iPhone locked. Unlock it to carry on.”), waits, and carries on
  where it stopped; plugging the cable in is enough. A big copy over Wi-Fi recommends the cable up front.
- **Several games in one go**: each game has its own progress and says what it waits for; one game failing
  doesn't stop the others, and **Retry** carries on from the game that stopped, even after a quit.
- **Update Sauce**: the device's Sauce version next to the latest release, and a button that builds the new
  one on your Mac and installs it over the old one. Saves, settings and games stay; a failed build leaves the
  working Sauce as it was.
- **Sauce shows a setting-up screen** instead of a black screen while a game starts: the name, a turning ring,
  what it's doing, and the version.
